How I use AI in projects.

Examples of using AI to support analysis, software development, documentation and troubleshooting—with human review and evidence checks included in the process.

My repeatable workflow

  1. Define the outcome. State the task, environment, constraints and success criteria.
  2. Provide evidence. Share relevant data, code, screenshots, errors or project files—excluding private details.
  3. Work in manageable steps. Ask for one practical next step or a specific type of analysis.
  4. Apply the suggestion. Make the change in the real project or environment.
  5. Verify. Run the program, inspect calculations, compare claims with the source, or check the resulting status.
  6. Document honestly. Record what succeeded, what was tested, limitations and remaining work.

Responsible use

  • Verify calculations and technical claims before publication.
  • Label fictional or synthetic data clearly.
  • Do not treat correlation as causation.
  • Keep passwords, private data, student information and internal configurations out of prompts and public repositories.
  • Distinguish suggested output from work actually implemented and tested.

Example: ScreenPulse prompt

“Act as a social media data analyst. Analyse the supplied eight-post dataset. Identify the format with the highest average engagement rate, the posts with the highest total engagements and reach, compare formats, and recommend three practical actions. Use only the actual figures, do not invent explanations, distinguish observations from hypotheses, and explain the limitations of the small sample.”
