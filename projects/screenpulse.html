Project overview

ScreenPulse Entertainment is a fictional portfolio company. The sample contains eight posts across Video, Image and Carousel formats. The goal was to demonstrate a responsible analysis workflow, not to make claims about a real brand.

Scope limitation: The sample is small (n=8) and fictional. Results are illustrative and directional; they do not establish causation.

Workflow: data to recommendation

  1. Prepare data in Excel. Start with format, reach, likes, comments, shares and saves.
  2. Calculate metrics. Total engagements = likes + comments + shares + saves. Engagement rate = total engagements ÷ reach.
  3. Compare formats. Use format-level averages and a chart to make differences visible.
  4. Ask AI to interpret. Use a structured prompt asking for patterns, practical recommendations, and limitations.
  5. Verify manually. Check figures against the workbook, remove unsupported interpretations, and distinguish observations from hypotheses.
  6. Communicate. Present findings and suggested tests in a concise report.

Carousel had the highest average engagement rate in this sample. Video post P08 had the highest reach (18,000) and the most total engagements (1,440), with an 8.00% engagement rate. Highest reach and highest engagement rate are different measures.

Because only two Carousel and two Image posts were included, these patterns are reasons to test further—not proof that a format will always perform better.

Recommendations

  1. Publish more Carousel posts and compare results across a larger sample.
  2. Review the creative choices in high-reach Video P08, treating possible explanations as hypotheses.
  3. Run a fair comparison across similar topics and posting conditions. Track engagement rate, reach, shares, saves and comments; consider clicks or conversions when available.

What this demonstrates

This case study documents a human-in-the-loop workflow: spreadsheet calculations, structured prompting, source checking, cautious interpretation, and practical recommendations. AI helped with interpretation and drafting; the analyst remained responsible for validating the figures and final claims.

Prompt approach
