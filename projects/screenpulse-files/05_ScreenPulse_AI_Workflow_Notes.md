# AI Workflow Portfolio

## Purpose
This document records examples of using generative AI as a practical assistant for analysis, software development, documentation and IT troubleshooting. AI output is treated as a suggestion to review, not as automatically correct.

## Repeatable workflow
1. Define the task, environment, constraints and success criteria.
2. Provide relevant evidence such as data, code, screenshots, errors or project files, while excluding private information.
3. Work in manageable steps and ask for a specific deliverable.
4. Apply suggested changes in the actual project or environment.
5. Verify results by running the program, checking calculations, comparing claims with source data or inspecting system status.
6. Record what was completed, what was tested, what remains uncertain and what should happen next.

## ScreenPulse: Excel and AI-assisted analysis
**Task:** Analyse eight fictional social media posts across Video, Image and Carousel formats.

**Excel work:** Calculated total engagements as likes + comments + shares + saves; calculated engagement rate as total engagements divided by reach; compared average engagement rate by format.

**AI support:** Used ChatGPT with a structured prompt to compare formats, identify posts with the highest reach and total engagements, draft an executive summary, and suggest practical next tests.

**Prompt approach:** “Act as a social media data analyst. Analyse the supplied eight-post dataset. Identify the format with the highest average engagement rate, the posts with the highest total engagements and reach, compare formats, and recommend three practical actions. Use only actual figures, do not invent explanations, distinguish observations from hypotheses, and explain limitations from the small sample.”

**Human verification:** Checked total engagement and engagement-rate calculations, compared format averages against Excel, checked top-post claims, removed unsupported claims about why a post performed well, and labelled ScreenPulse and its sample data as fictional.

**Verified sample findings:** Carousel averaged 8.50% across two posts; Video averaged 8.21% across four posts; Image averaged 7.25% across two posts. Video post P08 had reach of 18,000 and 1,440 total engagements, with an 8.00% engagement rate. There were 7,270 total engagements across the eight posts; the overall average post engagement rate was 8.04%.

**Recommendations:** Test additional Carousel posts; review high-reach Video P08 as a hypothesis-generating example; compare formats across similar topics and posting conditions, using engagement rate as the primary measure.

**Limitations:** The dataset is fictional and contains only eight posts. Topic, timing, impressions, clicks, conversions and audience demographics are not available. No causal claims are warranted.
