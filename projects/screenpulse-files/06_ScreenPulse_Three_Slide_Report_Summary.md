# ScreenPulse Entertainment — Social Media Analytics Report

**Portfolio exercise · fictional data · eight posts**

## Slide 1 — Overview

**Objective:** Understand engagement patterns and identify what to test next.

**Executive summary:** Carousel led on average engagement rate in this small sample; Video delivered the strongest reach and the highest total engagements in a single post.

- Total engagements: 7,270
- Average post engagement rate: 8.04%
- Highest post reach: 18,000 (Video P08)

**Definitions**
- Total engagements = likes + comments + shares + saves
- Engagement rate = total engagements ÷ reach

The findings are directional only: eight posts do not establish causation.

## Slide 2 — What the data says

| Format | Average engagement rate | Posts |
|---|---:|---:|
| Carousel | 8.50% | 2 |
| Video | 8.21% | 4 |
| Image | 7.25% | 2 |

**Key findings**
1. Carousel had the highest average engagement rate in the sample.
2. Video P08 reached 18,000 and generated 1,440 total engagements.
3. P08's engagement rate was 8.00%; reach and engagement rate measure different things.

Caution: the dataset contains only eight posts; findings are indicative, not conclusive.

## Slide 3 — What to do next

1. **Test more Carousels.** Publish additional Carousels and compare rates against other formats over a larger sample.
2. **Study high-reach Video.** Review P08's topic, opening and creative choices as hypotheses to investigate, not established explanations.
3. **Run a fair comparison.** Compare similar topics and posting conditions. Track reach, engagement rate, and engagement types.

**AI-assisted workflow**
1. Prepare metrics in Excel.
2. Prompt AI to compare formats and draft insights.
3. Verify figures and remove unsupported claims.
4. Turn validated findings into testable actions.

**Measurement plan:** Primary metric: engagement rate. Secondary metrics: reach, shares, saves and comments. Review the results after a larger sample is collected.

## Limitations

The dataset is fictional and includes eight posts. It has no topic, timing, impressions, click, conversion or audience-demographic data. Results do not prove that a content format caused a performance outcome.
